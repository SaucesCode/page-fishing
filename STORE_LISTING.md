## Detailed Description

Cast a line into any website you browse! Page Fishing turns your ordinary browser pages into tranquil, interactive ocean shallows where wildlife drifts across the screen as you read and scroll.

Click a passing creature to cast your cork-handled rod. Watch your rod tip flex, the line curve, and the fish leap into your floating bucket.

### Key Features

- **28 Handcrafted Marine Species**: From common Guppies and Angelfish to elusive Jellyfish, Seahorses, Manta Rays, Reef Sharks, and the legendary Moon Whale.
- **Dynamic Catch Mechanics**: Small fish require 1 quick hookset, medium creatures require 2 clicks, and apex titans (Sharks and Whales) demand a 3-click tug-of-war.
- **Tackle Boutique**: Earn pearls on every catch and customize your gear:
  - 5 Rods (Classic Birch, Bamboo Cane, Copper Shore, Moonstone, and Abyssal Carbon with rotating 3D gyro-auras).
  - 4 Lines (Monofilament, Golden Twine, Starlight Silk, and Neon Aqua laser beams).
  - 4 Buckets (Seafoam Tin, Woven Creel, Cedar Barrel, and Pearl Basin with orbiting pearl relics).
  - Specialty Baits (Glowing Krill and Golden Nectar).
- **Addictive Sensory Feedback**: High-energy impact strike shockwaves, bouncy `+Pearls` floaters, bucket squish-and-stretch landings, and organic synthesized Web Audio effects.
- **Atmospheric Weather & Night Mode**: Switch between Daylight, Moonlit Night with creature bioluminescence, and Gentle Rain with water ripples.
- **Automatic Dark Mode Adaptation**: Intelligent page luminance scanning dynamically ensures crystal-clear contrast and legible typography on light or dark websites.
- **Permanent Field Guide & Album**: Paginated specimen index tracking discovered species, rarity tiers, catch lengths, personal best records, and polaroid keepsakes.
- **Floating Companion Pet**: Choose any fish in your bucket to swim beside you as an orbiting virtual companion.
- **Full Privacy & Lightweight**: Runs entirely client-side with clean vector SVGs. Zero external API calls, zero ads, zero cookies, zero bloat.

### How to Use

1. Click the **Page Fishing** icon in your browser toolbar (or press **Alt+Shift+F**).
2. Scroll through any webpage to encounter swimming fish.
3. Click any fish to cast your rod and reel it in.
4. Click your bucket at the top right to view your catches, tackle shop, field guide, and discovery rewards.
5. Drag your rod and bucket anywhere on screen to suit your browsing layout.
