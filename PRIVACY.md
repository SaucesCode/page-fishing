# Privacy Policy for Page Fishing

**Last Updated**: October 31, 2026

Page Fishing is designed with complete privacy in mind.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
Page Fishing does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, browsing history, keystrokes, IP addresses, or identifiable user information.

### 2. Local Storage Only
All game progress—including caught fish, discovered species, earned pearls, and equipped cosmetic finishes—is saved entirely locally on your device using `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your browser.

### 3. Permissions
- `activeTab`: Used exclusively to display the interactive fishing canvas on the current page when you click the extension icon or press Alt+Shift+F.
- `scripting`: Executes the local SVG fishing interface in your current tab upon your explicit command.
- `storage`: Saves your in-game fish collection and settings locally on your computer.

### 4. No Remote Code & No Third Parties
Page Fishing contains zero remote scripts, analytics trackers, advertising networks, or external telemetry. All logic is self-contained within the extension package.

### Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out via GitHub: https://github.com/SaucesCode/page-fishing
