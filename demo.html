Page Fishing / A playable sketch

The internet has
a little wildlife.

Keep browsing. Something colorful might swim past.

Click a fish to cast your line. Watch it wriggle, fly up, and plop into the bucket beside your rod.

Drag your rod and bucket anywhere. Click the bucket to open your field guide.

A different kind of scrolling.

Fish drift through the page instead of living inside a game window. Scroll down and more visitors will swim into view.

A tangerine puffer. A pearl angelfish. Maybe something wearing a crown.

Different silhouettes, patterns, fins, and colors make each encounter a little different.

Little fish. Long journeys.

Each catch remembers the website where you found it. Your bucket becomes a small travel diary of the web.

Click the pause button beside the rod if you want the fish to stay still. The close button clears your page.

Your next fishing spot: the ocean

One more scroll?

Discover twenty-eight sea creatures, from guppies and jellyfish to turtles, dolphins, and the elusive moon whale, in four rarity tiers. Open the bucket to release your catch or browse your permanent fish index.

This demo saves to this browser. The installed extension shares a bucket across websites.
